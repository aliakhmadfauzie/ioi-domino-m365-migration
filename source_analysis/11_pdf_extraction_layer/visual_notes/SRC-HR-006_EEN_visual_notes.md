# Visual Notes — EEN.pdf (SRC-HR-006)

> **Form**: Employee Exit Notification | **Pages**: 8 | **Department**: HR

## Structure

**HR SECTION** (pages 1-2): Company/Site, Main Category, Category, Type, ContractStatus, Employee details (Name, Email, EmpNo, Position, Section), Exit Interview indicator, Notification dates, Resignation date, Last Working/Service dates, Superior (HOD), Division Head, Remarks

**Deactivation Management** (pages 2-3): System access deactivation date, extension capability ("Extend Date" button), Justification, Granted by

**APPROVE REJECT** (page 3): Status, EDName, EDDate, Remarks — Title "APPROVE REJECT"

**EXIT INTERVIEW** (pages 3-5): 5 numbered questions:
1. Reasons for leaving (with "otherReason" free text)
2. Progress feedback
3. Pay feedback
4. New job found?
5. New job comparison

**ACTUAL LAST WORKING DAY** (page 6): ActualLWD, Notify IT, Sent by, SentDate

**Hidden Fields**: CurrentAction, CA, Editor1-3

**Note**: This is the largest HR form (8 pages, 6,683 chars). Multi-section with exit interview questionnaire and IT notification.
